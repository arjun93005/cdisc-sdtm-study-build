# Protocol Synopsis - ZX-201 (fictional)

> Simulated study written for a clinical-programming portfolio. ZX-101 and ZX Therapeutics do not exist.

| Item | Description |
|---|---|
| Title | A Phase II, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled, Parallel-Group Study of ZX-101 in Adults with Stage 1-2 Essential Hypertension |
| Phase | II (dose-ranging) |
| Design | Randomised 1:1:1, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel group; site-stratified block randomisation (block size 6) via IxRS |
| Population | Adults 18-75 years with essential hypertension, mean seated SBP 140-179 mmHg at screening |
| Sites | 6 sites in USA (3), Germany (2), India (1) |
| Planned N | 180 randomised (60 per arm) |
| Treatments | Placebo, ZX-101 10 mg, ZX-101 20 mg, one oral tablet once daily for 12 weeks |
| Schedule | Screening (Day -14 to -1), Day 1, Weeks 2, 4, 8, 12, Follow-up 14 days after last dose |
| Primary endpoint | Change from baseline in mean seated SBP (mean of triplicate readings) at Week 12 |
| Secondary endpoints | Change from baseline in mean seated DBP at Week 12; proportion with SBP < 140 mmHg at Week 12 |
| Safety | Adverse events (MedDRA), clinical laboratory (central lab: chemistry, haematology), vital signs, concomitant medications |
| Key procedures | Triplicate seated BP after 5 minutes rest; Day 1 assessments performed **before** first dose; central labs at Screening, Day 1, Week 4, Week 12 |
| Key eligibility | Inclusion 3: seated SBP 140-179 mmHg. Exclusion 6: serum creatinine above protocol limit |
